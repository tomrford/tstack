# Sloppy copy

Interfaces should communicate through clear structure and controls, with text that earns its place. Remove copy that merely restates what a heading, control, or surrounding context already makes obvious.

### Redundant supporting copy

Do not routinely pair headings with subheadings or explanatory sentences. A heading should normally stand alone. Add supporting text only when it supplies information someone needs to understand the screen.

Example of bad copy (avoid at all costs):

```
HOME PAGE
Your window to the world.

SUBSCRIPTIONS
The channels you want, all in one place.

UPLOAD
Public, unlisted, or private. You control who sees your videos, on your time.

SHORTS
One-of-a-kind personalized feed, according to your tastes.
```

None of these subheadings add any value, they take up space, and they read like slop from a mile way. fight your instincts to write these; _always_ err on the side of too little copy when unsure.

### Icons over text

Apply the same test to text next to buttons, links, and actions: remove descriptions that just repeat the action. Prefer a clear, familiar icon for a simple action when its meaning is evident from context.

For example, a trash icon is better than a text button containing the text 'delete', and in turn does not need a nearby sentence saying it deletes an item.
